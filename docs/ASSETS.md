What the logo actually is (verified at 100%):
- A heavy, wide geometric sans **"WARM"**, then **"UP" knocked out of a rounded rectangle**. The rectangle's top edge breaks into a small **upward arrow notch** above the "U".
- **"VEIN ENHANCER SLEEVE"** sits beneath "WARM" inside the artwork, in the same heavy style. It is about 9.6% of the logo height, so at a 200 px wide header it is about 4.8 px tall. Sizing implications are covered in DESIGN_PLAN §2.
- **A continuous left-to-right gradient.** Column means sampled from opaque pixels at 1 / 17 / 33 / 50 / 66 / 83 / 99% of the width:
  `#0D31B0 → #2232A3 → #58357F → #7E3767 → #A6394C → #C23B39 → #DD3C27`.
  These agree with the brief's stops (#0F31AF … #4A3488 … #953857 … #DB3C28). The middle is a **muted plum**, not a clean blue-to-coral blend.
- The "UP" letters are **transparent**, so the page surface shows through. The logo therefore only goes on white or very pale neutrals.

**Keying finding:** B − G (after a σ = 2 px blur) separates sleeve from mat cleanly.

| Region | B − G range |
|---|---|
| Fleece | +7 to +15 |
| Knit | +5 to +16 |
| Hook-and-loop | about +22 |
| Mat, including glare and dark grid lines | −16 to −2 |
| Ruler | about −24 |

Thresholding at +1.5, keeping the largest component and filling holes gives a clean silhouette. The blue-grey tabletop strip at the far left (B − G ≈ +3) is separated by the ruler and drops out.

**Scale.** Used only internally to keep the mitten's proportions believable. **No dimension is published anywhere.**

### Thumb side

In the worn photo the strap is on the arm's right and the thumb emerges at the lower left of the knit section. In the flat lay the strap attaches along the **top** edge, so the thumb belongs on the **bottom** edge of the hand section, about 65% of the way from the wrist seam to the open end.

## 4. Product document

Facts used: purpose (warming the arm and hand before blood draws and IV placement), materials (high-quality fleece and cotton), the attached Velcro strap (written as "hook-and-loop" on the site, a generic term), heat-pack pouches, and that the patient keeps the sleeve and may reuse it during an inpatient stay.

**Excluded, never used or paraphrased:**
- "It's a scientific fact that warming… improves success rates."
- "…perceived as less painful…"
- "…to increase heat for even better success at venipuncture."
- "A number of heat packs" becomes "heat packs", with no count.
